  WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
  -----------------

  1. ZC reviews application (typically 1-3 days).

  2. ZC assigns a node number (3323:1/N) and generates a
     shared secret.

  3. ZC sends both back to you by reply email or netmail.

  4. You configure your tosser per HUB.TXT and connect.

  5. Welcome to CheeseNet.


  REJECTIONS
  ----------

  Applications may be declined. Reasons include but are not
  limited to:

   - Unverifiable BBS (no public hostname or contact)
   - History of policy violations on other networks
   - Identity already in active use elsewhere on CheeseNet

  If declined, you will receive a netmail or email explaining
  why and (where applicable) what would be needed to reapply.
